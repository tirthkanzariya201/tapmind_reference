Select a **Feature**, then narrow by **Product**, **Framework**, **Platform**, and **Mediation**. Results are split by product (Custom Adapter GMA SDK and Custom Adapter GMA Next-Gen SDK), never combined across products.

<Info title="Legend">
- <Badge color="green" size="sm" shape="pill">Shipped</Badge> confirmed shipped
- <Badge color="gray" size="sm" shape="pill">Shipped, unconfirmed</Badge> shipped but not yet verified
- <Badge color="gray" size="sm" shape="pill">Not applicable</Badge> feature does not apply to this package
- <Badge color="red" size="sm" shape="pill">Not yet built</Badge> not shipped in this package yet
</Info>
